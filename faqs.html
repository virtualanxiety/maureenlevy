Practical Information Fees & FAQs

How much does a session cost?
Individual sessions are €85 and couples sessions are €130.

How can I pay?
Payment can be made by bank transfer or cash.

Can I claim through my health insurance?
Many Irish Life Health and Laya Healthcare plans include benefits for counselling and psychotherapy. I can provide a receipt for you to submit to your insurer. Please check your individual policy for the level of cover available.

Do you offer an initial consultation?
Yes. I offer a free 15-minute consultation, giving you an opportunity to make initial contact, ask any questions and discuss whether you would like to arrange a session.

Who do you work with?
I work with individuals and couples, including adults and students, and also have experience working with groups.

Do you offer online counselling?
Yes. Counselling is available in person, online and by telephone.

Is the practice wheelchair accessible?
The current practice location is not wheelchair accessible. Online and telephone counselling are also available.
